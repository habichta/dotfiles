# Reminders

Lines are markdown checkboxes with an optional leading ISO date. Dated entries
sort by urgency on the start screen and are tagged `overdue` / `today` / `Nd`;
undated entries trail behind. Anything further out than
`g:startify_reminders_horizon` days is hidden until it gets close.

- [ ] 2026-08-03 Example: something due soon
- [ ] Example: no date, just don't forget
